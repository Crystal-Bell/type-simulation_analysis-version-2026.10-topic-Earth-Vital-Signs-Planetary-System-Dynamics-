Key Lever Performance Projections
 * Lever A: Rapid Fossil Fuel Subtraction Rate
   * Action: Coupling renewable expansion directly with legally binding phase-out dates and absolute emission caps.
   * Impact: Reduces global warming acceleration from 0.36\text{°C}/\text{decade} down to <0.15\text{°C}/\text{decade} by 2035.
 * Lever B: Short-Lived Climate Pollutant (SLCP) Abatement
   * Action: Targeting targeted leaks in global methane infrastructure and agricultural nitrous oxide.
   * Impact: Yields an immediate reduction in net atmospheric warming over a 5–10 year window, reducing peak temperature overshoot.
 * Lever C: Deforestation Reversal & Tropical Sink Protection
   * Action: Expanding international land-use protection (expanding on the 36% tropical primary forest loss reduction achieved recently).
   * Impact: Stabilizes land-based carbon sinks, directly curbing Earth's net energy imbalance.
3. High-Impact Action Plan
 * Shift Subsidies & Phase Out Fossil Fuels ($1.2T/yr Reallocation)
   * Redirect energy subsidies globally toward long-duration storage, smart grid integration, and clean transition mechanisms.
 * Implement Direct Methane Mitigation ($200B Global Target)
   * Enforce strict real-time satellite monitoring and mandatory leak-repair protocols for fossil extraction and distribution pipelines.
 * Institutionalize Tropical Primary Forest Safeguards
   * Formalize global economic frameworks compensating rainforest nations for permanent ecosystem preservation.
